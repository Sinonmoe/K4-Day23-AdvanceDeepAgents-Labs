# Survey on Video and Multimodal Generation

## TL;DR
- Multimodal generation involves integrating diverse input modalities, enhancing coherence and interaction across multiple formats [1][2][3][4].
- Key architectures such as diffusion models and multimodal transformers are critical for improving generative output quality [5][6][7].
- Major applications span entertainment, education, and real-time interaction capabilities, highlighting the importance of user-centric design in video generation [8][9][10].
- Open challenges focus on improving temporal consistency, evaluation metrics, and safety mechanisms in multimodal frameworks [11][10][8].

## Background
Multimodal generation is a dynamic field focused on creating content that seamlessly integrates various forms of data—such as text, images, and audio. Foundational work identifies key areas such as modality heterogeneity, alignment, and the interaction between different data types that need robust theoretical backing [1][3].

## Theoretical Foundations
The theoretical underpinnings of multimodal generation are explored through frameworks like FlowInOne, which integrates various input modalities into cohesive visual outputs. Other models, such as those combining diffusion methods with large language models, highlight the necessity of flexible systems capable of dealing with diverse inputs [5][6][2][3]. These frameworks aim to address challenges around representation, alignment, and generation across modalities, essential for developing effective video generation systems.

## Key Architectures and Methods
Significant architectures in this space include WOVEN, which enhances multimodal reasoning through improved training paradigms, and CamViG, which incorporates camera awareness for video generation tasks [7][12]. Frameworks like DataVista and InteractiveVideo represent steps toward user-centric generation, allowing for more dynamic content creation driven by multimodal inputs [13][11]. The evolution of these methods represents a continual shift towards sophisticated, integrated systems capable of simultaneously processing and generating content across multiple formats.

## Major Applications and Benchmarks
The applications of these technologies are far-reaching, influencing sectors like entertainment and education. The establishment of benchmarks, such as the VBench and the emerging T2V-CompBench, facilitates the assessment of generative models based on key criteria, including visual fidelity and semantic alignment [9][10]. Studies underscore the versatility of models, demonstrating their effectiveness in real-world scenarios, such as autonomous driving and personalized content generation [11][10].

## Trends and Open Problems
Despite advancements, the field faces challenges, including ensuring model safety and logical reasoning, particularly in video generation where consistency and multimodal alignment are crucial [10][8]. Existing benchmarks reveal limitations in multimodal models' capacities, highlighting the need for further refinement of evaluation metrics and improved integration of multimodal data streams for enhanced user experience [11][9][8].

This survey illustrates the complexity and rapid evolution of video and multimodal generation, emphasizing the importance of theoretical, practical, and safety considerations as the field continues to advance.
